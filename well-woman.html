1. Overview: The Power of Preventive Care

Preventive gynaecological care is the foundation of lifelong female health. Many major women’s health conditions—including cervical cancer, breast lumps, pelvic infections, and hormonal disorders—develop quietly over months or years without causing noticeable symptoms.

Under the compassionate guidance of Dr Prapti Shah, regular well-woman checkups empower women through evidence-based screening, early detection, HPV vaccination, and open clinical discussions about menstrual, sexual, and reproductive wellness across every decade of life.

Preventive Gynaecology: Anatomical visualization of the cervix, squamocolumnar transformation zone, and cervical cytology screening.

2. Recommended Screening Milestones

Ages 21 to 29

  • Cervical Pap Smear Cytology every 3 years to screen for precancerous cervical changes
  • Annual pelvic examination and clinical evaluation of menstrual regularities
  • HPV Vaccination (if not completed in adolescence)
  • Contraceptive counseling and pre-conceptional health planning

Ages 30 to 65

  • Co-Testing: Pap Smear combined with High-Risk HPV DNA testing every 5 years (or Pap smear every 3 years)
  • Annual Clinical Breast Examination (CBE) and breast self-awareness education
  • Screening mammography every 1 to 2 years starting at age 40 (or earlier if family risk exists)
  • Metabolic, thyroid, lipid, and bone mineral density surveillance

3. When to Seek Urgent Clinical Care

Seek Immediate Evaluation If You Notice:

  • Post-coital bleeding (bleeding after intercourse) or intermenstrual bleeding: Requires immediate speculum examination and cervical evaluation to rule out cervical lesions.
  • Postmenopausal bleeding (any bleeding after menopause): Always considered an urgent clinical red flag requiring endometrial sampling and ultrasound.
  • A new, painless, firm breast lump or nipple retraction/discharge: Demands immediate triple assessment (clinical exam, ultrasound/mammography, and core biopsy).

4. Cervical Cancer Screening & HPV Prevention

Cervical Cancer Is Almost 100% Preventable: Nearly all cervical cancers are caused by persistent infection with high-risk strains of Human Papillomavirus (HPV strains 16 and 18). Because precancerous changes take 5 to 15 years to develop, routine screening detects cellular dysplasia early when it is completely curable.

Liquid-Based Cytology (LBC): A gentle, painless cervical swab collected during a speculum examination to examine cells from the cervical transformation zone.

High-Risk HPV DNA Testing: Highly sensitive molecular testing identifying high-risk viral DNA before cellular changes even appear.

HPV Vaccination: Highly effective prophylactic vaccine protecting against high-risk oncogenic HPV strains. Recommended for adolescent girls, young women, and eligible adult women up to age 45.

5. Breast Health Awareness

  • Clinical Breast Examination (CBE): Routine palpation of breast tissue and axillary lymph nodes during annual checkups.
  • Breast Self-Awareness: Knowing what is normal for your breasts and promptly reporting changes in contour, skin dimpling, or nipple discharge.
  • Screening Mammography: Low-dose digital X-ray screening capable of detecting microcalcifications years before a lump becomes palpable.

6. What Happens During a Well-Woman Visit?

A well-woman visit is gentle, comfortable, and respectful:

  • Confidential Health Discussion: Review of menstrual history, family medical history, lifestyle, and contraceptive questions.
  • Vital Health Metrics: Blood pressure check, BMI assessment, and screening for anemia and thyroid balance.
  • Gentle Pelvic Examination & Pap Swab: Takes less than two minutes with minimal discomfort.
  • Personalized Prevention Roadmap: Clear timeline for future screenings, vaccinations, and lifestyle recommendations.

7. Managing Precancerous Findings (Colposcopy)

If a Pap smear shows abnormal cells (such as ASCUS or LSIL/HSIL), a Colposcopy provides magnified examination of the cervix with gentle acetic acid application. Minor in-office procedures (such as LEEP or cervical biopsy) remove precancerous cells completely, preventing cancer from ever developing.

8. Lifelong Wellness Commitment

Prioritizing an annual well-woman visit is one of the single most proactive investments in your health, ensuring peace of mind, early detection, and lifelong vitality.

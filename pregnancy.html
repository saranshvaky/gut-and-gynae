1. Overview: Comprehensive Maternity Care

Pregnancy is a transformative and deeply personal journey. Structured, compassionate obstetrical care ensures the health, safety, and emotional well-being of both mother and baby from the moment of conception through labor, delivery, and the postpartum transition.

Led by Dr Prapti Shah, our maternal-fetal approach strongly advocates for gentle, evidence-based normal delivery whenever safe, paired with vigilant monitoring protocols to detect and manage high-risk pregnancy conditions early.

Maternal-Fetal Development: Medical illustration depicting progressive fetal development and maternal physiological adaptation across trimesters.

2. Trimester-by-Trimester Roadmap

First & Second Trimester Focus

  • First Trimester (Weeks 1–12): Dating ultrasound, maternal blood panels, folic acid optimization, and Nuchal Translucency (NT) scan for chromosomal screening
  • Second Trimester (Weeks 13–28): Detailed Target Fetal Anomaly Scan (TIFFA / Level-II USG), cervical length screening, and maternal glucose tolerance test

Third Trimester & Labor Preparation

  • Third Trimester (Weeks 29–40): Growth scans, fetal umbilical Doppler, biophysical profile, and group B streptococcus screening
  • Birth Planning: Pelvic assessment, labor pain management guidance, birth plan discussions, and breastfeeding education

3. When to Seek Urgent Clinical Care

Seek Immediate Evaluation If You Notice:

  • Vaginal bleeding or sudden fluid leakage: Requires immediate emergency obstetrical evaluation to rule out placental abruption, placenta previa, or premature rupture of membranes.
  • Persistent severe headache, vision changes, or sudden facial swelling: Classic warning signs of gestational hypertension or preeclampsia requiring prompt blood pressure check.
  • Decreased or absent fetal movements in the third trimester: Demands immediate non-stress test (NST) and fetal biophysical evaluation.

4. High-Risk Pregnancy Care

Gestational Diabetes Mellitus (GDM): Expert maternal glucose monitoring, personalized dietary guidance, and medical therapy when needed to ensure optimal fetal growth and prevent macrosomia.

Hypertensive Disorders & Preeclampsia: Vigilant blood pressure management, fetal growth tracking, and uterine artery Doppler screening to safeguard maternal and fetal health.

Multiple Gestation (Twins): Specialized ultrasound monitoring for monochorionic/dichorionic twin pregnancies to ensure balanced fetal development.

Previous Cesarean Section (VBAC): Structured evaluation and trial of labor after cesarean (TOLAC) for suitable candidates seeking normal vaginal birth.

5. Diagnostic & Ultrasound Protocol

  • First Trimester Dating & Viability Scan: Confirms intrauterine pregnancy, gestational age, and cardiac activity.
  • Combined First Trimester Screening (NT Scan + Dual Marker): Assesses risk of fetal chromosomal anomalies.
  • Level-II Anomaly Scan (18–20 Weeks): Comprehensive anatomical survey of fetal organs, spine, brain, heart, and limbs.
  • Color Doppler Growth Scans: Evaluates fetal placental blood flow (umbilical artery and middle cerebral artery) in high-risk pregnancies.

6. Normal Delivery Advocacy & Labor Care

We believe birth is a natural physiological process. Our labor suite emphasizes:

  • Patient-First Normal Birth Advocacy: Encouraging spontaneous onset of labor, upright positioning, gentle mobility, and continuous emotional support.
  • Pain Relief Options: Non-pharmacological coping techniques alongside round-the-clock labor epidural analgesia when requested by the mother.
  • Electronic Fetal Heart Rate Monitoring (CTG): Continuous monitoring ensuring baby’s safety throughout active labor.
  • Emergency Cesarean Preparedness: Immediate surgical readiness when medical indications (fetal distress, cephalopelvic disproportion) make operative birth necessary for mother or baby safety.

7. Postpartum Care & Lactation Support

Comprehensive postpartum recovery including pelvic floor rehabilitation, gentle emotional check-ins for postpartum mood health, professional lactation guidance, and newborn feeding support.

8. Pre-Conceptional Counseling

Planning for pregnancy begins before conception. Pre-conceptional evaluation assesses rubella immunity, thyroid function, hemoglobin levels, and genetic family history, initiating folic acid supplementation early for optimal neural tube protection.
